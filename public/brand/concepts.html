Direction A

The Broadsheet

"A financial publication with teeth."

Warm paper, deep ink, serif headlines — reads like a premium research desk, not a hacker dashboard. Fox orange is the brand voice; money green is reserved for gains and BUY signals, so green always means money. Builds the most trust per pixel.

The Night Den

"Foxes hunt at night."

My recommendation

A in the daylight, B after dark

Build Direction A (The Broadsheet) as the core identity — it earns the trust a money brand needs, it's the furthest from the derivative terminal look, and it ages well. Then make the site's dark mode Direction B (The Night Den) rather than a boring inverted palette: same layout, but at night the fox hunts. One brand, two moods, and the dark mode itself becomes a shareable feature.

Logo: #2, the Tail Chart — it's the only mark that says both "fox" and "markets" in one stroke, and no one else has it.
