# Company and ETF destinations

Local implementation, 13 September 2026. Not yet published.

## Delivered

- Homepage sector stock links and sector-page company cards open company overviews.
- Each overview identifies the company using the existing SEC security master, names the sector, and has breadcrumbs and return navigation.
- House records are separate from the company identity and restricted to the matching ticker and issuer.
- All 22 ETFs in the sector directory have individual ETF overviews with names, sponsors, sector focus, sourced expense figures, source dates and an illustrative annual expense.
- ETF links no longer open an undifferentiated list or a House disclosure page.
- Explore remains active in navigation for sector, stock and ETF routes.
- No demonstration prices, returns, holdings or rankings are imported into these pages.

## Sources and meaning

- [State Street sector overview](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/capabilities/equities/sector-investing/select-sector-etfs): 11 equity sector funds; published suite expense ratio 8 bps. Individual fund source links are in the catalog.
- [Vanguard sector prospectus](https://www.vanguard.com/pub/Pdf/p954.pdf): 10 sector ETF fee tables, 0.09% annual operating expenses, with page-specific links. Dated December 19, 2025, supplemented June 30, 2026.
- [VNQ summary prospectus filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/734383/000073438326000136/f45323d1.htm): 0.13%, dated May 28, 2026.

The expense illustration is $10,000 multiplied by the annual percentage, assuming constant value. It excludes brokerage costs and taxes. These dated reference facts are not a subscribed daily fund-data feed or a grant of API redistribution rights.

## Still required — do not describe the ETF experience as complete

- Production holdings ingestion with report dates, effective dates, fund series/class identity and recorded source terms.
- Holdings weights, concentration, fund assets and related-stock exposure.
- Licensed or otherwise appropriately sourced price/total-return histories, distributions and corporate actions.
- Refresh, stale-data detection, corrections, and coverage reporting.
- Holdings-based comparisons and rankings only after those inputs are connected and verified.
- Shared image-library work remains separate and unfinished.

Do not substitute the sector's editorial stock list for a fund holdings list. Do not interpret absent coverage as zero holdings or zero returns. Do not populate these pages from the gated demonstration provider.
