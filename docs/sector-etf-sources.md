# Sector ETF directory — first release

Checked 13 September 2026. This is an editorial directory of two examples per sector, not a fund ranking, holdings feed, or endorsement. Facts recorded: ticker, provider, sector association. Fund labels are shortened display labels, not a security-master identifier for joining holdings.

- State Street's [official sector ETF directory](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/capabilities/equities/sector-investing/select-sector-etfs) identifies all eleven Select Sector SPDR equity funds and their sectors. Premium-income products are excluded.
- Vanguard's [U.S. Sector ETFs prospectus](https://www.vanguard.com/pub/Pdf/p954.pdf), dated 19 December 2025 and supplemented 30 June 2026, identifies ten sector ETF share classes on its cover.
- Vanguard's [sector ETF overview](https://investor.vanguard.com/investment-products/etfs/sector-etfs) identifies VNQ for real estate in addition to VDE and VHT.

No logos, fund descriptions, charts, marketing copy, performance series, or holdings files are copied. Links lead to the issuer evidence. No account, purchase, sponsor placement, or paid referral is involved. Public access is not treated as a blanket redistribution license.
