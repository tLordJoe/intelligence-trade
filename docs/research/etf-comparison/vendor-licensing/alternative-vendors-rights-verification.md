# Market-Data Vendor Rights Verification — Public Anonymous Multi-ETF Comparison Tool

**Use case verified against:** an advertising-supported commercial website (Outfox Markets) publishing a comparison tool for VOO, QQQ, XLK, SMH, XLU, SOXX to anonymous, non-logged-in visitors, using 10 years of daily adjusted price history, distributions, splits, ETF reference data (expense ratio, NAV, net assets, inception date) and complete holdings, and displaying derived analytics (total return, growth of a visitor-entered dollar amount, volatility, maximum drawdown, holdings overlap, best-performer ranking).

**Verification date:** September 5, 2026. Every value below comes from a page fetched in this session; the URL is linked next to the value. Where a page did not answer a question, the report says **written vendor confirmation required** rather than inferring an answer. Marketing/pricing-page statements are labelled separately from contract language, and conflicts are called out.

**No purchase recommendation is made.**

### Termination / downgrade / data deletion — can derived results be preserved?
- 6.2 Effect of Termination: Customer must "(i) cease use of The Services, including all Data or information contained in or **derived from** The Services; (ii) destroy or … return … all copies or other embodiments" ([ToS](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service)).
- 6.3 Data Deletion: delete all Data "**including data cached**" and "sign the Data Deletion Agreement in Exhibit A", with FMP audit rights "should Financial Modeling Prep suspect that Customer is continuing to use the Data".
- Narrow carve-out in §5: "provided however, if the Agreement is not terminated for cause, the Customer may retain copies of the reports or information printed or obtained through The Services subject at all times to the license restrictions specified in Section 2.4 and the non-disclosure requirements of this Section 5".
- Consequence for this use case: on termination, published derived analytics must come down, because 6.2(i) reaches information "derived from" the Services. Whether the §5 retention carve-out permits continued *display* of previously computed results is contradictory on its face (6.2 says cease use of derived information; §5 permits retaining copies) → **written vendor confirmation required**.
- Downgrade specifically: not addressed by 6.2/6.3. But 2.10 Right to Monitor lets FMP "downgrade", suspend or terminate unilaterally, and 2.8 requires notifying FMP of IP/domain aliases with FMP able to audit domains ([ToS](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service)). Effect of a *voluntary* downgrade on retained data: **written vendor confirmation required**.

### Verdict
FMP **cannot** lawfully power a public anonymous comparison tool on any self-serve tier: ToS 2.2.2 prohibits showing FMP data on a website absent a specific agreement, and the pricing page states the same requirement. A compliant path exists in principle — the commercial Enterprise tier described as covering "data display and redistribution" plus the Data Display and Licensing Agreement — but its price and scope are entirely unpublished, and two contract features are adverse to this use case even then: mandatory deletion of cached and derived data on termination (6.3/6.2) and the bar on naming FMP as the data source (10.4).

### Verdict
Polygon/Massive is the only vendor examined whose published contract affirmatively grants what this use case needs: §2.2 licenses use of the Information in the Customer's own websites, §6.1(e) expressly extends to "Edge Users" (the customer's end users, with no login requirement), and the Stocks Business tier at a published $2,499/mo is marketed and contracted as carrying "Commercial & display rights". - **Derived data is favourable, where it qualifies:** "'Derived Data' shall mean any information generated in whole or in part from the Information such that the information generated cannot be reverse engineered or decompiled to recreate the Information, or be used to create other data that is recognizable as a reasonable substitute"; and "Nasdaq makes no proprietary claim to any Derived Data" (§§1.8, 4.3(b)). A chart of daily closes would generally not qualify as Derived Data under that definition.
- **Caching/storage:** licensed only during the term — "license to receive use, process and **store** the Information during the term of this Agreement" (§4.1(a)). **No express deletion-on-termination clause** appears; only "rights and obligations… that, by their nature, are intended to survive… will survive" (§10.8) → **written vendor confirmation required**.
- **ETF data:** no ETF holdings or ETF reference dataset was identified. The product page references ETP valuation data via GIDS, Nasdaq Fund Network "performance, NAV, valuation, and strategy-level reference data", and Sharadar with "insider and institutional holdings" and data "going back over 20 years" ([product page](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/data/data-link)) — none confirmed as complete ETF holdings. Adjusted-price semantics, distributions and splits coverage: not stated on any fetched page → **written vendor confirmation required**.
- **Pricing, DPA, SLA, exchange-fee schedule:** none published → **written vendor confirmation required**.

**Verdict:** Nothing in Nasdaq Data Link's published documentation confirms a right to display its data on a public anonymous website; the operative Global Data Agreement is structured around Distributors and Service Orders, requires pre-approval for API-based distribution, and may require the website operator itself to contract directly with Nasdaq. No price, no ETF holdings dataset, and no adjusted-history specification could be verified. On published evidence it cannot be shown to support this use case.

## 7. Marketing-versus-contract conflicts (explicit)

1. **EODHD** — the pricing page markets tiers such as ALL-IN-ONE at $99.99/mo as full data access, but each is labelled personal use and the operative [Terms](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/terms-conditions) prohibit displaying the Information; the help article states that commercial use is licensed separately ([Commercial vs Personal](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/commercial-vs-personal-license-use)). The Terms are the operative document; the commercial-plan display scope lives in an unpublished Data Services Agreement.
2. **FMP** — no conflict on display: the pricing footnote and ToS 2.2.2 agree that a separate Data Display and Licensing Agreement is required ([Pricing plans](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/pricing-plans), [ToS](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service)). The conflict is internal to the ToS: 6.2(i) requires ceasing use of information "derived from" the Services on termination, while §5 lets a customer not terminated for cause "retain copies of the reports or information printed or obtained through The Services". The ToS is stated to be the operative agreement, subject to any Order Form.
3. **Polygon/Massive** — marketing says "SLAs available in our enterprise plans" ([polygon.io/business-stocks](https://www.polygon.io/business-stocks)) while a full 99.8% SLA is published and incorporated into the [Businesses ToS](https://massive.com/legal/businesses-terms-of-service) by reference ([SLA](https://massive.com/legal/businesses-terms-of-service-sla)); the contract governs. Marketing also says corporate actions go "back to 2008" ([massive.com/stocks](https://massive.com/stocks)) while endpoint docs say dividends from 2000 and splits from 1978. And the ETF Global partner page markets $99/mo datasets that are "Individual use only", so the price a commercial display user pays is not the advertised one ([partner page](https://massive.com/partners/etf-global), [constituents docs](https://massive.com/docs/rest/partners/etf-global/constituents)).
4. **Intrinio** — pricing-page phrases like "full display rights" (for indices) and "Display-quality… real-time data" ([Pricing](https://intrinio.com/pricing)) sit against ToS §2.2, under which Display is prohibited except as granted in an executed Order Form, which "shall control" ([ToS](https://about.intrinio.com/terms)).
5. **Nasdaq** — the product page markets broad distribution and data monetisation ([product page](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/data/data-link)) while the Global Data Agreement prohibits any use not expressly described in a Service Order and requires pre-approval for API-based distribution ([GDA](https://assets.ctfassets.net/mx0rke14e5yt/6DYAXkkVqSVwEZVvOMLJwN/5ba36a22dd18d28845837b2bb83c6fc9/Global_Data_Agreement_Terms_and_Conditions_5.0_8_15_2025.pdf)).

Per the brief, no "commercial use allowed" phrasing on any pricing page has been treated as answering Q1 or Q10; in every case the verdict above rests on contract language or on an express display statement.

## 8. Per-vendor verdicts (can it lawfully power a public anonymous comparison tool?)

- **EODHD — No, on published terms.** Self-serve tiers are personal use and expressly bar displaying the Information; the $399/mo Internal Use commercial tier expressly bars display outside the company. A path may exist via Enterprise ($24,990/yr) or Custom, but the display right is nowhere published and the governing Data Services Agreement is not public.
- **Financial Modeling Prep — No, on published terms.** ToS 2.2.2 prohibits showing FMP data on any website absent a specific agreement, "irrespective of whether such usage is complimentary or paid". The commercial Enterprise tier is described as covering "data display and redistribution" but is unpriced. Even with that agreement, 10.4 forbids naming FMP as the data source and 6.2/6.3 compel ceasing use of derived data and deleting cached data on termination.
- **Polygon.io / Massive — Yes in principle, on the Stocks Business plan, subject to three open items.** The published Businesses ToS grants use of the Information in the customer's own websites and expressly contemplates Edge Users (the customer's end users), and the Business tier is published at $2,499/mo with commercial and display rights, backed by a published 99.8% SLA. Open items: unpriced ETF Global business licences for holdings/expense ratio/NAV/net assets/inception; split-only vendor adjustment, meaning total-return series are customer-computed and must be cleared against the open-ended §6.1(j) derivative-works bar; and mandatory deletion of all Information on termination with the status of already-published derived results unaddressed.
- **Intrinio — Cannot be confirmed from published documents.** Display and commercial use are prohibited by the base Terms and available only under a Startup or Enterprise Order Form that controls over the Terms and is not public. The undefined bar on operating a "competing… financial information offering" is a specific risk for a financial content website.
- **Nasdaq Data Link — No, on published terms.** The operative Global Data Agreement is a distributor framework: any use not expressly described in a Service Order is prohibited, API-based distribution requires Nasdaq pre-approval, and website operators displaying a Distributor's service may be required to contract with Nasdaq directly. No pricing, no confirmed ETF holdings dataset, and no adjusted-history specification could be verified.
