## 2. Fees — quoted fee-table lines

### SMH

Verbatim from the summary prospectus dated **February 1, 2026** ([VanEck](https://vaneck.onlineprospectus.net/VanEck/MOB_library/MOB_data/LIB_SummaryProspectus/SMHSUM/SMHSUM.pdf); identical table in the [SEC-filed summary prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137360/000113736026000117/vanecksemiconductoretfsmh4.htm)):

> **Annual Fund Operating Expenses** (expenses that you pay each year as a percentage of the value of your investment)
> Management Fee — **0.35%**
> Other Expenses(a) — **0.00%**
> Total Annual Fund Operating Expenses(a) — **0.35%**
>
> (a) Van Eck Associates Corporation (the "Adviser") will pay all expenses of the Fund, except for the fee payment under the investment management agreement, acquired fund fees and expenses, interest expense, offering costs, trading expenses, taxes and extraordinary expenses. **Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Adviser has agreed to pay the offering costs until at least February 1, 2027.**

- Gross expense ratio: **0.35%**; Net expense ratio: **0.35%** — as-of the fact sheet dated **August 31, 2026** ([VanEck fact sheet](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/investments/semiconductor-etf-smh-fact-sheet.pdf)).
- Fee waiver: **no fee waiver line in the fee table**. The only time-limited undertaking is the Adviser's agreement to pay *offering costs* until at least **February 1, 2027** (unitary-fee structure: gross = net).
- Portfolio turnover: 12% of average portfolio value in the most recent fiscal year ([SEC summary prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1137360/000113736026000117/vanecksemiconductoretfsmh4.htm)).

### SOXX

Verbatim from the summary prospectus dated **July 31, 2026** ([iShares](https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/summary-prospectus/sp-ishares-phlx-semiconductor-etf-3-31.pdf)):

> **Annual Fund Operating Expenses** (expenses that you pay each year as a percentage of the value of your investment)¹
> Management Fee — **0.33%**
> Other Expenses — **0.00%**
> Total Annual Fund Operating Expenses — **0.33%**
>
> ¹ Operating expenses paid by BFA under the Investment Advisory Agreement exclude acquired fund fees and expenses, if any.

- Expense example: 1yr $34 / 3yr $106 / 5yr $185 / 10yr $418. Portfolio turnover 33% ([same source](https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/summary-prospectus/sp-ishares-phlx-semiconductor-etf-3-31.pdf)).
- Fund page confirms **0.33%**, labelled "Expense Ratio: Fees as stated in the prospectus" ([iShares fund page](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239705/ishares-semiconductor-etf)); the Fees tab shows "Management Fee 0.33% / Acquired Fund Fees and Expenses 0.00% / Other Expenses + 0.00% / Expense Ratio 0.33%".
- Fee waiver: **no contractual waiver**. The statutory prospectus states only a voluntary provision: *"BFA may from time to time voluntarily waive and/or reimburse fees or expenses in order to limit total annual fund operating expenses… Any such voluntary waiver or reimbursement may be eliminated by BFA at any time."* ([iShares prospectus](https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/prospectus/p-ishares-phlx-semiconductor-etf-3-31.pdf)). **No expiry date is stated.**

> **Discrepancy flagged (surprise #1):** three iShares primary documents fetched today state three different expense ratios, reflecting a stepped-down fee and stale artefacts on the issuer's own CDN — **0.35%** in the statutory prospectus PDF dated *August 1, 2024* ([link](https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/prospectus/p-ishares-phlx-semiconductor-etf-3-31.pdf)), **0.34%** in the fact sheet PDF at the canonical fact-sheet URL ([link](https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/fact-sheet/soxx-ishares-semiconductor-etf-fund-fact-sheet-en-us.pdf)), and **0.33%** in the July 31, 2026 summary prospectus and on the live fund page. The 0.33% figure is the current one. Note all three PDFs sit at *undated, permanently-rewritten* URLs ("…-3-31.pdf"), so a cached copy cannot be distinguished from the current one by URL alone.

### SOXX (iShares)

- **Publishes daily full holdings: yes** — the fund page exposes "Detailed Holdings and Analytics" and "Data Download Excel, opens in a new tab" ([iShares fund page](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239705/ishares-semiconductor-etf)).
- **Exact URL tested:** `https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239705/ishares-semiconductor-etf/1467271812596.ajax?fileType=csv&fileName=SOXX_holdings&dataType=fund` (and the legacy `…/ishares-phlx-semiconductor-etf/…` variant).
- **HTTP behaviour tested 2026-09-05 — bot-gated, and gated deceptively:**
  - Both URL variants return **HTTP 200** with `Content-Type: text/csv;charset=UTF-8` and ~1.41 MB of payload — but the body is **HTML, not CSV**. It begins `<!DOCTYPE html><html …>` and its `<title>` is `iShares Semiconductor ETF | SOXX`, i.e. the fund landing page is served under a CSV content type.
  - This is invariant across: no User-Agent, a Chrome browser User-Agent, a cookie jar seeded from the fund page, and `Referer` + `Accept: text/csv` headers. Every permutation returned the same HTML body under `text/csv`.
  - **Practical consequence:** an automated client gets a *false success* — status 200 and a CSV MIME type — while receiving zero holdings rows. Naïve pipelines will silently ingest HTML.

- `robots.txt` at [https://www.ishares.com/robots.txt](https://www.ishares.com/robots.txt) blocks `Brightbot 1.0` entirely, and for `User-agent: *` disallows `/*?truepdf*`, `/*?norepdf*`, `/*.dl$`, `/search/`, and the sign-on endpoints. The `.ajax?fileType=csv` holdings endpoint is **not** listed in robots.txt — the gate is enforced at the application layer instead.

## B) Does either issuer permit a third-party commercial website to republish their fund data or holdings?

**No. Both prohibit it, and both require prior written/express permission.**

**VanEck — controlling clause** ([https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/legal/](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/legal/)):

> "No information contained on this site may be reproduced, transmitted, displayed, distributed, published or otherwise used for commercial purposes without the prior consent of VanEck. You may, however, print or electronically store copies of the information for your own personal use."

This is a flat prohibition on commercial reproduction, display, distribution and publication of *any* information on the site — which includes holdings, NAV, AUM, distributions and performance. The only carve-out is personal use.

**BlackRock / iShares — controlling clauses** ([https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/compliance/terms-and-conditions](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/compliance/terms-and-conditions)):

> "You may not distribute, modify, transmit, reuse, repost, or use the content of this Website for public or commercial purposes, including all text, images, audio, and video, without BlackRock's written permission."

> "Such content is solely for your personal, non-commercial use. Accordingly, you may not copy, distribute, modify, post, frame or deep link this Website, including any text, graphics, video, audio, software code, user interface design or logos."

And, separately, the collection method is prohibited:

> "Use any robot, spider, intelligent agent, other automatic device, or manual process to search, monitor or copy this Website or the reports, data, information, content, software, products services, or other materials on, generated by or obtained from this Website, whether through links or otherwise (collectively, "Materials"), without BlackRock's permission, provided that generally available third-party web browsers may be used without such permission;"

BlackRock's terms bar the *acquisition* (automated collection) and the *publication* (commercial redistribution, deep linking, framing) separately — a commercial site would need permission on both counts.

**Index layer adds a second, independent prohibition.** Even a site that obtained issuer permission would still need index-provider licences to publish index levels or constituents:
- MarketVector §4.1(c)/(d): no derived charts or reports, no reproduction or distribution of Data ([TOS](https://www.marketvector.com/terms-of-service)); §4.2(a) allows MarketVector to impose a **retrospective** licence fee on breach.
- ICE Data: "Index Data is only made available to Subscriber for its own internal use and Index Data cannot be redistributed or published in any form to any third party without the prior written consent of ICE Data" ([ICE T&Cs](https://www.ice.com/publicdocs/IDI_-_Terms_and_Conditions_for_the_Index_Data_and_Custom_Index_Services.pdf)); the website-display permission is limited to a *subscriber* displaying end-of-day values about *its own* funds.

**Practical conclusion:** neither SMH nor SOXX fund data may lawfully be republished by a third-party commercial website on the strength of the public pages alone. A compliant path requires (a) written permission from VanEck / BlackRock respectively, and (b) a separate data licence from MarketVector / ICE Data for anything index-related. The SEC EDGAR filings cited throughout this report (N-PORT, 485BPOS, 497) are US Government public-domain records and are the redistribution-safe alternative for holdings and fee data, at the cost of lower frequency (quarterly rather than daily).
