# Primary-Source Data Profile: VOO and QQQ

Research date: 2026-09-05. Every value below carries a link to the exact page fetched in this session. Values that could not be confirmed from a primary source are marked `n.a.`

**Headline surprise up front:** QQQ is **no longer a unit investment trust**. Effective after market close on **December 19, 2025**, Invesco QQQ Trust, Series 1 was reclassified from a UIT to an **open-end management investment company**, and its expense ratio dropped from 0.20% to 0.18% ([SEC 485BPOS, 2025-12-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000110465925123273/tm2529177d1_485bpos.htm)). Conversely, **VOO does not publish a daily full holdings file** — it is not a Rule 6c-11 standalone ETF but an ETF share class of a multi-class open-end fund operating under a Vanguard exemptive order, and its holdings-disclosure policy is **monthly with a 15-calendar-day lag** ([Vanguard SAI](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/36405/000003640526000181/f45032d1.htm)).

## 12. Direct answers

### A) Is QQQ's unit investment trust structure a problem for a fund-comparison tool?

**Not any more — the premise is out of date as of 2026-09-05.** QQQ ceased to be a UIT after the close on **December 19, 2025**: "Effective after market close on December 19, 2025, the Fund was reclassified as an open-end management investment company (the 'Reclassification'). Prior to the Reclassification, the Fund operated as a unit investment trust ('UIT')," following beneficial-owner approval of amendments to the Initial Trust Indenture on December 19, 2025 ([485BPOS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000110465925123273/tm2529177d1_485bpos.htm)), confirmed in the semi-annual report ([N-CSRS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000119312526250483/8deb6f921189c0e.htm)).

What the UIT structure *did* lack, per the filings, and which QQQ now has:

1. **No investment adviser and no management fee construct.** "The Trustees considered that the Fund was previously structured and operated as a unit investment trust ('UIT Fund') that **did not have an investment adviser**, and that the Adviser served as the UIT Fund's sponsor, without compensation…" ([N-CSRS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000119312526250483/8deb6f921189c0e.htm)). Consequently there was **no Form N-1A fee table** with "Management Fees / Other Expenses / Total Annual Fund Operating Expenses" — an EDGAR full-text search for that exact phrase across CIK 0001067839 for 2024-01-01 to 2025-06-30 returns **zero hits**, versus a full fee table in the 2025-12-19 485BPOS.
2. **No series/class identifiers.** The Series ID `S000101292` and Class ID `C000271435` appear only with the post-reclassification filing ([EDGAR filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000110465925123273/0001104659-25-123273-index.htm)); a UIT has no series/class taxonomy for a comparison schema to key on.
3. **No board of directors/trustees, no adviser-level oversight, and a different report set** — the trust historically filed N-30B-2 periodic reports; it now files Form N-CSR/N-CSRS and N-PORT: "The Trust's Forms N-PORT and Forms N-CSR will be available on the SEC's website at www.sec.gov." ([485BPOS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000110465925123273/tm2529177d1_485bpos.htm))
4. **No portfolio-management flexibility** — a UIT must hold the index constituents essentially mechanically and cannot use the sampling/optimization or securities-lending latitude available to a managed fund; Invesco marketed the change as "Enhanced transparency and oversight" ([DEFA14A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1067839/000119312525227142/d33270ddefa14a.htm)).

**Practical implication for a comparison tool:** the historical time series is discontinuous. Pre-2026 QQQ rows carry a 0.20% expense figure that is not a 485BPOS fee-table number, no series/class IDs, and no N-1A-shaped fields; post-2025-12-19 rows do. Any schema must date-stamp the structure field rather than treat it as static. A residual asymmetry versus VOO remains but runs the other way: **VOO** is the one with the odd shape — it is a share class of a multi-class open-end fund, so its "fund" AUM ($1,675,038M) and its "ETF" AUM ($978,960M) differ by a factor of 1.7 ([fact sheet](https://fund-docs.vanguard.com/F0968.pdf)), and it does not publish daily holdings, while QQQ (now a 6c-11 standalone fund) does.

### B) Does either issuer permit a third-party commercial website to republish their fund data?

**No. Both prohibit it, and both also prohibit the automated collection you would use to obtain it.**

Vanguard, controlling clauses ([Vanguard Terms and Conditions](https://investor.vanguard.com/terms-conditions)): users may not "Modify, copy, screen capture, distribute, forward, transmit, post, display, perform, reproduce, publish, broadcast, license, create derivative works from, transfer, sell, or exploit any reports, data, information, content, software, RSS and podcast feeds, products, services, or other materials on, generated by or obtained from this Site, whether through links or otherwise"; the licence granted is for "personal, informational, and noncommercial use"; "The Materials on this Site are for information, education, and noncommercial purposes only"; and users may not use "devices or software that provide repeated automated access to this Site, other than those made generally available by Vanguard."

Invesco, controlling clauses ([Invesco Terms of Use](https://www.invesco.com/us/en/resources/terms-of-use.html)): users may not "Resell or provide commercial use of the Website or the content therein, or create any derivative use of the Website or the content therein; or use any data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction tools on the Website"; may not "Reproduce, duplicate, copy, sell, resell, visit, or otherwise exploit for any commercial purpose the Website without the express prior written consent of Invesco"; and the licence is "limited, non-exclusive and revocable … to access and make personal use of the Website."

Index layer, same answer: Nasdaq grants a licence "solely for your personal, non-commercial use" and requires "prior written approval" to "share, transfer, disclose, copy, publish or create derivative works from the content" ([nasdaq.com/legal](https://www.nasdaq.com/legal)); SPDJI requires "express prior written consent" to "copy, reproduce, modify, display, perform, publish, distribute, transmit, broadcast, circulate, create derivative works from, store, or link to" its site or Content, and treats website display of real-time index values as a licensed activity ([SPDJI terms of use](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/terms-of-use/), [SPDJI licensing](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/about-us/data-index-licensing/)).

The compliant paths that remain: (i) SEC EDGAR filings (N-1A/485BPOS, N-PORT, N-CSR) — public-domain US government records, which is where most of the hard numbers in this report came from; (ii) a negotiated data licence with each issuer and each index owner; or (iii) a licensed commercial market-data vendor.

## 13. Gaps and caveats

- **QQQ dynamic fields (AUM, holdings count, expense ratio, 30-day median bid-ask spread) are not obtainable from invesco.com in an automated way.** `www.invesco.com` returns **HTTP/2 406, empty body, `cache-control: private, no-store`** to direct requests, including the CSV holdings download endpoint, even with a desktop-Chrome User-Agent. A rendering fetcher gets the shell and the static distribution/premium-discount tables but returns "Not stated" / "Unable to load data" for the live statistic tiles. All QQQ size and holdings figures here therefore come from SEC filings.
- **Invesco's served distribution and premium/discount tables appear stale**, ending at 09/22/2025 and Q/E 09/30/2025 respectively. Post-09/2025 QQQ distributions: `n.a.`
- **Vanguard's investor-site data panels are client-side rendered** and return `broken_content_ip_and_js` to fetchers; the underlying `etfportal.vanguard.com/extrapi/v2/us/standard/` host returned 404 on all guessed paths. Vanguard's PDF documents (`fund-docs.vanguard.com/p968.pdf`, `F0968.pdf`) and `advisors.vanguard.com` pages are cleanly fetchable at HTTP 200 and carry most of the needed values.
- **Not confirmed:** VOO 1940 Act structure-related split history; VOO 2026 quarterly per-share amounts by ex-date; QQQ 1940 Act file number; QQQ 30-day median bid-ask spread.
- `https://fund-docs.vanguard.com/portfolio-holdings-disclosure-policy.pdf` and `https://fund-docs.vanguard.com/etf-distribution-schedule.pdf` are **Vanguard Funds plc (Irish UCITS)** documents and are *not* applicable to VOO; they were deliberately excluded.
