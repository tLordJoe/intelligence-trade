### Index methodology change — VERIFIED FROM FILINGS

The Select Sector index weighting rules **were changed effective October 4, 2024**, and the change is documented in a definitive prospectus/SAI supplement.

| | Before (SAI dated January 31, 2024, as revised March 4, 2024) | After (Supplement dated October 4, 2024) |
|---|---|---|
| Single-stock cap | "…may not exceed **25%** of the total value of its respective Select Sector Index" ([SAI 2024-03-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524055178/d788397d497.htm)) | "…may not exceed **24%** of the total value of its respective Select Sector Index" ([497 supplement 2024-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524232956/d835962d497.htm)) |
| Step 1 redistribution | "each Component Stock that exceeds 24% … will be reduced to **23%** … and the excess amount will be redistributed proportionally across the remaining Component Stocks that each represent less than 23% … repeated as necessary until no company breaches the 23% weight cap" ([SAI 2024-03-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524055178/d788397d497.htm)) | "each Component Stock that exceeds 24% … will be reduced to 23% of the total value of the Select Sector Index." ([497 2024-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524232956/d835962d497.htm)) |
| Step 2 (the >4.8% / 50% bucket) | "the first Component Stock to cause the 50% limit to be breached will be reduced to **4.5%** and the excess amount will be distributed proportionally across all remaining Component Stocks that represent less than 4.5% …" ([SAI 2024-03-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524055178/d788397d497.htm)) | "the weight of such Component Stocks will be **reduced proportionately so that the sum of such Component Stocks represent 45%** of the total value of the Select Sector Index," followed by a new third and fourth step re-applying excess weight to stocks below 4.5% ([497 2024-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524232956/d835962d497.htm)) |
| Stated reason | — | "on account of certain changes to the constituent weighting rules for the respective benchmark index of each series of the Trust" ([497 2024-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312524232956/d835962d497.htm)) |

The current SAI carries the post-change 24% / 23% language ([485BPOS 2026-01-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312526027312/d15107d485bpos.htm)). The prospectus describes the index as calculated on a "**modified market capitalization**" methodology, "which means that modifications may be made to the market capitalization weights of single stock concentrations in order to conform to the requirements of the Internal Revenue Code" ([485BPOS 2026-01-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312526027312/d15107d485bpos.htm)). An EDGAR full-text search restricted to CIK 0001064641 for the phrase "constituent weighting rules" returns exactly one filing — the October 4, 2024 supplement ([EDGAR full-text search API](https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index?q=%22constituent+weighting+rules%22&ciks=0001064641)); note EDGAR full-text search only covers 2001 forward, so pre-2001 methodology changes cannot be excluded from that search alone.

## J. Anything surprising — pipeline hazards

1. **The daily NAV history file is NOT split-adjusted.** XLK goes 291.043301 (04-Dec-2025) → 146.61729 (05-Dec-2025), with shares outstanding jumping 325,805,897 → 650,611,794; XLU goes 87.406771 → 43.291728 ([navhist XLK](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/navhist-us-en-xlk.xlsx); [navhist XLU](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/navhist-us-en-xlu.xlsx)). Any naive return series built from this file records a **−49.6% one-day loss** for XLK on 2025-12-05. Meanwhile the audited financial highlights in the same period **are** retroactively split-adjusted ([N-CSR/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312526031265/d81465dncsra.htm)) — so the issuer publishes adjusted and unadjusted per-share histories simultaneously.
2. **The distribution history file is also unadjusted.** XLK's ex-09/22/2025 dividend is 0.357867 and ex-12/22/2025 is 0.218827 ([spdr-etf-historical-distributions.xlsx](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/spdr-etf-historical-distributions.xlsx)); the drop is mostly the 2-for-1 split, not a payout cut. Dividend-growth and trailing-yield calculations will be wrong unless the 2025-12-04 factor is applied.
3. **XLK's distribution history has holes.** The file jumps from ex-12/17/1999 straight to ex-12/20/2002, then to 09/19/2003, and shows several 0.000000 quarters in 2004–2007 ([spdr-etf-historical-distributions.xlsx](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/spdr-etf-historical-distributions.xlsx)). XLU's history, by contrast, is continuous back to 03/19/1999. Do not assume symmetric coverage across tickers in the same file.
4. **The NAV history starts 01-Dec-2003, five years after inception**, and the Shares Outstanding / Total Net Assets columns are literally `"-"` for the oldest rows ([navhist XLK](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/navhist-us-en-xlk.xlsx)). "Since inception" returns on the site cannot be reconstructed from the published NAV file.
5. **Holdings files contain negative weights and non-equity lines.** XLK carries "XAK TECHNOLOGY SEP26" index futures at **−0.002655%** and "US DOLLAR" at **−0.00477%**; XLU carries "XAU UTILITIES SEP26" at **−0.012493%** ([holdings XLK](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/holdings-daily-us-en-xlk.xlsx); [holdings XLU](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/products/fund-data/etfs/us/holdings-daily-us-en-xlu.xlsx)). A pipeline that sums weights, sorts descending, or assumes equities only will misbehave. The `Sector` column is present but filled with `"-"` for every row.
